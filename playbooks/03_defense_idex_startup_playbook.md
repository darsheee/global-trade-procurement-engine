# Playbook 03: Sovereign Defense Startup & iDEX Grant Capture Engine

## 1. The Lean Defense Playbook: Software & COTS Edge-AI
Traditional defense contracting requires decades of heavy metal fabrication, gunsmithing, and massive testing ranges. 
In the modern drone-saturated battlefield (Ukraine/Middle East/Indo-Pacific), the high-leverage defensive edge has shifted to:
1. **Acoustic Drone Detection Nodes**: Arrays of MEMS microphones powered by edge neural networks running on $50 Raspberry Pi / Jetson modules to classify drone motor frequencies at >300m range (immune to radar jamming and optical camouflage).
2. **Autonomous Terminal Homing Kits**: Micro-computer vision add-on boards for FPV loitering munitions to lock onto targets in GPS-denied and heavily jammed electronic warfare environments.

## 2. Non-Dilutive Government Capital: iDEX DISC Challenges
The Indian Ministry of Defence through **iDEX (Innovations for Defence Excellence)** awards non-dilutive grant funding to recognized startups and MSMEs:
- **DISC (Defence India Startup Challenge)**: Grants of **₹1.5 Crores** (DISC) up to **₹10 Crores** (iDEX Prime) with 50% milestone matching.
- **Fast-Track Procurement (Make-II)**: Following successful prototype validation, the Armed Forces place single-vendor development and prototype purchase orders with zero tender competition.
- **Capital Needed**: Zero heavy capex. Software simulation, CAD design, COTS microelectronics, and AI agent prompt-orchestration provide the working prototype.
